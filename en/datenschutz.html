VEILORA (iPhone and iPad)

Privacy Policy

Applies to the game “VEILORA – Lost Among Stars” for iPhone and iPad. Last updated: November 2026.

In short

VEILORA stores your progress only on your device. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no user account. We do not collect personal data. The game only needs an internet connection if you choose to start online co-op with friends.

What the game stores on your device

  • your progress: levels, in-game currencies, ships, upgrades, home base, missions and achievements;
  • your settings, such as volume, language, display filters and engine color;
  • which story scenes and hints you have already seen;
  • whether “Ad-Free” (Werbefrei) has been purchased – just yes or no; the App Store remains the authority;
  • a pilot name of your choice, if you enter one.

This data never leaves your device and is not collected by us.

Online co-op (optional)

Only when you create a co-op room or join one with a room code does the game connect directly (peer-to-peer) to your friend’s device. To set up the connection it uses the public PeerJS broker (0.peerjs.com) and connection servers (STUN by Google, TURN by PeerJS). Technically your IP address and a random room code are visible to these services; your co-op partner sees your IP address and pilot name. Only game data such as positions and hits is exchanged. We store none of it. Without co-op the game makes no internet connections.

In-app purchase

The game is free. The only purchase is “Ad-Free” (one-time, no subscription), processed by Apple through the App Store under Apple’s own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). The game never sees payment details; it only learns whether “Ad-Free” has been purchased.

Deletion

Your progress is deleted when you remove the game. A purchase stays linked to your Apple Account; after reinstalling, “Restore Purchases” in the shop restores it.

What the game does not do

  • no ads, no ad or analytics services, no advertising identifier, no tracking;
  • no location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files;
  • no user accounts, no chat with strangers, no notifications, no subscriptions.

Play-money casino

The optional casino uses play chips only. Chips can never be bought with real money and winnings cannot be paid out.

Rights and contact

Depending on where you live, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and/or the EU GDPR apply. As we collect no data, we hold no data about you from the game. Complaints can be made to a data protection authority (in Switzerland the FDPIC, in the EU the authority of your country).
