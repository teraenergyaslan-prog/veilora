Help & Contact

Thanks for playing VEILORA! If something is missing or doesn’t work, just write to us.

Contact: teraenergyaslan@gmail.com
We usually reply within a few days. Please include your device, iOS version and a short description.

What is free, what costs money?

The whole game is free and has no ads. Everything can be earned by playing. The only purchase is “Ad-Free” (Werbefrei): one-time, no subscription.

What does “Ad-Free” do?

After each round you can optionally double and triple your reward. Without the purchase this starts a short bonus transmission (5 seconds with a game tip – not an ad). With “Ad-Free” there is no wait and double rewards are applied automatically.

Where do I find it? New device?

Lobby → Shop → department “Werbefrei”. “Käufe wiederherstellen” (Restore Purchases) is directly below. Your progress itself is stored only on the device.

Language

The game is mainly in German; English is being expanded with updates.

What does Early Access mean?

The game is fully playable and keeps growing with new chapters, ships, worlds and improvements. Your progress is kept. Feedback by email is very welcome.
